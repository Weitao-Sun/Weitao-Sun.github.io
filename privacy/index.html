Privacy Policy

Personal_EBAY_Test · Effective October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Personal_EBAY_Test application (the "Application"), developed and operated by Weitao Sun ("I", "me"), collects, uses, and protects information when it is authorized to access an eBay seller account through eBay's OAuth process.

1. Who the Application is for

The Application is a personal automation tool built to help manage my own eBay seller account (for example, listings, inventory, orders, and fulfillment). It is not offered as a public service, and no data is collected from the general public.

2. Information collected

When an eBay account owner grants consent, the Application may access the following through official eBay APIs, limited to the permission scopes approved during authorization:

  • Authorization data — OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by eBay. eBay passwords are never seen or stored by the Application.
  • Account information — seller user ID and basic account/business settings.
  • Listing and inventory data — items, prices, quantities, and listing status.
  • Order and fulfillment data — order details, and buyer name, shipping address, and contact details only as needed to fulfill orders.
  • Messages and feedback — where the corresponding scope is granted, to help respond to buyers.

3. How information is used

Information is used solely to provide the Application's features for the authorized seller account, such as automating listing updates, syncing inventory, processing orders, and generating reports. Information is not sold, rented, or used for advertising, marketing, or profiling.

4. Sharing and disclosure

Information is not shared with third parties, except:

  • with eBay, as required to perform API requests on the account owner's behalf;
  • with infrastructure providers (e.g., hosting) strictly to run the Application, under appropriate confidentiality and security obligations;
  • when required by law or to protect rights, safety, or security.

5. Storage and security

Data is stored only as long as and to the extent needed to operate the Application. OAuth tokens and any stored data are protected using reasonable safeguards, including encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS), restricted access, and keeping credentials out of source code. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but care is taken to protect the information.

6. Data retention and deletion

Data is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes above. When access is revoked or no longer needed, associated tokens and data are deleted within a reasonable period. The Application honors eBay's Marketplace Account Deletion/Closure notifications and deletes related personal data upon receipt.

7. Revoking access

You can revoke the Application's access at any time from your eBay account under Account settings → Sign in and security → Third-party app access. After revocation, the Application can no longer access your eBay data.

8. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information held by the Application by contacting me using the details below. Requests will be handled promptly and in accordance with applicable law.

9. Children's privacy

The Application is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction), and no information is knowingly collected from children.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: weitaosun@ucla.edu
